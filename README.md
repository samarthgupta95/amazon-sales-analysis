# Amazon Sales Analysis

An Excel-based data analysis project focused on evaluating sales performance, profitability, product categories, order statuses, payment methods, fulfillment methods, and geographic distribution.

## Project Overview

The objective of this project is to develop an interactive Excel dashboard that provides a clear overview of Amazon India's sales and profitability. The dashboard is designed to help non-technical stakeholders understand business performance through KPIs, charts, and interactive filters, supporting data-driven business decisions.

The analysis is structured around four key business objectives.

## Business Objectives

### 1. Overall Sales & Profit Performance
- Analyze total sales, total profit, and overall profit margin.
- Evaluate total orders, units sold, and average order value.
- Examine monthly sales and profit trends.
- Assess overall business performance over time.

### 2. Category & Product Performance
- Compare sales and profit across product categories.
- Identify the top 10 products by sales.
- Analyze category-level performance to identify revenue drivers.
- Identify opportunities for product-level growth.

### 3. Order Status & Revenue Loss
- Analyze delivered, shipped, returned, and cancelled orders.
- Calculate return and cancellation rates.
- Examine category-wise returns and cancellations.
- Identify opportunities to improve order completion and reduce returns.

### 4. Payment, Fulfillment & Geographic Performance
- Evaluate sales and profit across payment methods.
- Compare fulfillment methods based on sales and profitability.
- Analyze state-wise sales, profit, and order distribution.
- Identify key markets and operational performance patterns.

## Key Findings & Recommendations

### Key Findings

- **Overall Performance:** Generated approximately ₹15.58 crore in sales and ₹3.32 crore in profit, with an overall profit margin of 21.29%.
- **Category Performance:** Electronics & Mobiles contributed approximately 79.32% of total sales, making it the primary revenue driver.
- **Product Performance:** Laptop Backpack recorded the highest sales among individual products, followed by Power Bank 20000mAh and 5G Smartphone.
- **Order Status:** Cancelled and returned orders accounted for 9.85% of total orders, highlighting opportunities to improve order outcomes.
- **Payment Methods:** UPI contributed approximately 50.40% of total sales, making it the leading payment channel.
- **Fulfillment:** Amazon FBA accounted for approximately 69.02% of total sales.
- **Geographic Performance:** Maharashtra was the leading state by sales, followed by Karnataka and Delhi.
- **Sales Growth:** Sales increased by approximately 0.73% from 2024 to 2025, indicating relatively stable revenue with limited year-over-year growth.

### Recommendations

- **Improve Profitability:** Monitor profit margins and evaluate discount strategies to protect profitability while driving sales growth.
- **Diversify Revenue:** Explore growth opportunities in smaller product categories to reduce dependence on Electronics & Mobiles.
- **Reduce Returns & Cancellations:** Investigate cancellation and return reasons to identify recurring product, payment, and delivery issues.
- **Optimize Fulfillment:** Compare fulfillment methods based on profitability, delivery performance, and operational costs.
- **Strengthen Key Markets:** Prioritize inventory planning and targeted marketing in high-performing states while exploring opportunities in other markets.
- **Improve Sales Growth:** Use monthly sales trends to identify high-performing periods and develop targeted promotional strategies.
- **Monitor Business KPIs:** Regularly track sales, profit margin, return rate, cancellation rate, and order completion to evaluate business performance.

*Note: Sales associated with cancelled and returned orders represent revenue exposure and should not be interpreted as actual financial losses without further analysis.*

## Business Value

This project demonstrates how Excel-based data analysis and visualization can transform raw sales data into actionable business insights.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor business performance, identify key revenue drivers, evaluate profitability, assess order outcomes, and understand the contribution of different payment methods, fulfillment channels, and geographic markets.

## Conclusion

The project brings together key sales and operational metrics in a single Excel-based reporting solution. The findings highlight the importance of maintaining profitability, strengthening high-performing product categories, reducing returns and cancellations, and identifying opportunities for sustainable business growth.
